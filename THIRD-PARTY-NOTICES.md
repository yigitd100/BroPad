The vendor's current "VB-CABLE Distribution with another product" section allows
bundling the base package with an application, including installer integration,
provided users can identify VB-CABLE and support its donationware model. Those
attributions appear in BroPad's voice-chat setup screen and this notice.
Professional/organization distribution has separate vendor licensing conditions.
Paid additional cables A+B and C+D are not included.

about installer integration differs from the current website terms; retain both
and review the vendor's current terms before each public release. BroPad does not
relicense the driver under MIT or rename the driver as its own product.
